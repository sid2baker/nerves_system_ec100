The stock misc partition contains CRC-valid Rockchip AvbABData and the exact
U-Boot exposes bootavb command strings. Hash-gated boot_fit disassembly proves
that its CONFIG_ANDROID_AB block is disabled, so the default FIT path neither
selects a slot nor appends a slot suffix. A production A/B rootfs needs an
independent selector or a reviewed replacement U-Boot.
